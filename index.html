Formula 1

About F1!!

Formula 1 ( F1 ) is the world 's highest - level open - wheel motorsport competition, managed by the International Automobile Federation ( FIA ), and considered the pinnacle of speed, technology, and the most expensive competition on the planet.

History of Formation

  • Its predecessors were the European Grand Prix races of the 1920s and 1930s .
  • The term "Formula 1 " officially came into use in 1946 , defining a common set of rules for all participating cars and teams.
  • The first Formula 1 World Championship was held in 1950 at Silverstone, England, with Alfa Romeo driver Giuseppe Farina becoming the first champion.

Basic information about the tournament

  • Each season comprises approximately 20-23 races (collectively known as Grand Prix) held across continents ,on bothdedicated racetracks and city streets (the most famous being the Monaco Grand Prix)
  • Each participating team has two racing cars and two main drivers , with a maximum of four drivers allowed throughout the season.
  • The scoring system for the top 10 finishers is as follows: 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 points. If the race is stopped early, the points will be split in half.
  • Each stage of the race lasts a maximum of 2 hours , covering a total distance of approximately 300km.

Basic Laws and Terminology

  • The Q1 - Q2 - Q3 qualifying race takes place the day before the main race and determines the starting order
  • Rule 107%: A driver whose Q1 lap time is 107 % slower than the fastest driver will not be allowed to participate in the main race , unless otherwise permitted by the stewards.
  • The flag system is used to transmit signals on the racetrack : checkered flags signal the end of the race, yellow flags warn of dangerous situations and prohibit overtaking , red flags signal a stop to the race, and the Safety Car enters the track in case of an incident.
  • The operating costs of an F1 team can reach hundreds of millions of dollars annually , with revenue coming from race revenue sharing , sponsorships , and commercial partnerships.
